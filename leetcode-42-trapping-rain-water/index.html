Why the smaller boundary?

If lMax < rMax, the right side can hold water at least as high as lMax. Update lMax, then settle the left cell. Otherwise, settle the right cell using rMax. Equal maxima take the right branch, exactly as in your solution.

The green and purple guides show lMax and rMax (the largest walls seen on each side). The gold guide shows their minimum, the limiting boundary for the next cell. These are boundary maxima, not minimum column heights. Rain illustrates falling water; it is not a fluid simulation. Water appears only when the algorithm resolves a cell. Back restores the earlier water level. O(n) time · O(1) solver space; playback keeps snapshots.
